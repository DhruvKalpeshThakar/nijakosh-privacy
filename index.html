Nijakosh Privacy Policy

निजकोश · Last updated: 5 November 2026

Nijakosh is a password manager made by [YOUR NAME]. This policy explains what happens to your data.

What we collect

Nothing.

  • Your passwords, notes and settings are stored only on your device, encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key derived from your master key.
  • Nijakosh has no internet permission and does not send any data to us or anyone else.
  • We use no analytics, no ads and no tracking.

Backups

If you create a backup file, it is encrypted with your master key and saved only where you choose, for example your own Google Drive. We never receive it.

Fingerprint and face unlock

This is handled by Android. Nijakosh never sees or stores your biometric data.

Your master key and recovery code

We never see them and cannot reset them. If you lose both, your data cannot be recovered.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app or use “Delete vault” in Settings. You can delete any backup files you saved elsewhere yourself.

Children

Nijakosh is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
